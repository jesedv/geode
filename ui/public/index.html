No radicals. No numerics. No complex numbers.

Why geode

200+ year gap, solved

Since Abel–Ruffini (1824), nobody had a closed-form rational solution for quintics. Hyper-Catalan series changes that.

Exact rational arithmetic

No floating point in the solver path. BigInt coefficients, BigRational arithmetic. Truncate at any depth for the precision you need.

Works in your browser

The solver compiles to WASM. Type a polynomial, get the answer. No server, no install, no account.

Game physics, graphics, crypto

Quintic equations in cloth sim, Bézier intersection, implicit surfaces, and analysis of polynomial-based cryptographic primitives.

How it works

The Hyper-Catalan series gives:

x = Σₘ Cₘ · (a₁/a₀)^{m₁} · (a₂/a₀)^{m₂} · ...

where Cₘ are Hyper-Catalan numbers counting polygon dissections. Truncation at depth d gives O(d) correct digits.
